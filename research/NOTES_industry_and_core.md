**D1. FY26 finished steel production & consumption — vintage conflict**

  Finished steel production 160.94 Mt; consumption 163.74 Mt
- MoS "Development of Indian Steel Sector since 2014-15" (Jun-2026 vintage), citing JPC:
  Total finished steel production 161.74 Mt; consumption 164.19 Mt
- WHY: JPC provisional data is revised as returns are received under the Collection of Statistics Act 2008.
  The June-2026 document is the later vintage and also explicitly labels the series
  "Total Finished Steel (alloy/stainless + non-alloy)".
- RESOLUTION: use the LATER vintage (Jun-2026) for the long-run series in Demand & Consumption;
  retain the Mar-2026 MER vintage for the supply-balance (production+imports-exports-stock) because that
  table is internally consistent. Disclose both.
